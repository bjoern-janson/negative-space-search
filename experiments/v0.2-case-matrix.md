Status: **fixed before policy implementation or execution**

The held-out cases differ from development cases in capability names, failure counts, residual magnitudes, noise scales, and costs.

## Intended structural discriminator

The benchmark does not prescribe one algorithm, but the relevant distinction is:

```text
systematic residual persistence relative to expected noise
```

versus:

```text
residual behavior compatible with ordinary uncertainty/noise.
```

The held-out `held_U_1` case is intentionally same-sign across all failures but small relative to `sigma`; a simple same-sign heuristic should therefore false-escalate.

The frozen v0.1.1 negative-space policy is expected to over-escalate because every case reports unresolved ordinary hypotheses. That expected failure is a control, not a reason to alter the v0.1.1 policy.
