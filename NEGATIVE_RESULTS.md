# Negative Results and Claim Contraction — Negative-Space Search v0.7

Status: **frozen archival summary**.

The negative-result history is part of the research artifact. Stronger hypotheses that failed are preserved rather than rewritten as successful components of the final theory.

The canonical detailed ledger remains `results/negative-result-ledger.md`. This document compresses the main archival consequences.

## NR-001 — no demonstration of autonomous interface invention

The original model-inadequacy/interface boundary remained unresolved in the strong sense required for autonomous interface invention.

Later versions progressively improved localization, representation repair, basis composition, language-boundary detection, and construction inside a supplied meta-language. None demonstrated autonomous invention of a new primitive observation/interface substrate or synthesis generator without the relevant repair affordances being supplied.

Archival consequence:

> v0.7 must not be described as solving interface invention or autonomous representation invention.

## NR-002–NR-004 — generic alternatives remain live

Across the benchmark sequence, matched strong alternatives repeatedly removed apparently unique Negative-Space Search advantages:

- strong causal reasoning matched hostile causal interpretation once evidence access was matched;
- history-aware causal reasoning matched selective escalation once history-sensitive competence was matched;
- typed general causal history matched structured negative-space history when representation was matched;
- generic representation-repair and basis-repair learners reproduced the useful capability without a unique negative-space algorithm.

Archival consequence:

> Generic causal, representation-learning, structure-learning, and synthesis explanations remain viable competitors.

The project has not established a unique negative-space mechanism.

## NR-005 — equal invention quality under gated and always-expand synthesis

In v0.7, the boundary-gated and always-expand generic synthesizers constructed the same retained abstractions and achieved the same repair quality:

```text
Q_invention(boundary-gated) = 1.0
Q_invention(always-expand)  = 1.0

held-out transfer(boundary-gated) = 1.0
held-out transfer(always-expand)  = 1.0
```

The difference was search allocation:

```text
candidate evaluations(boundary-gated) = 5200
candidate evaluations(always-expand)  = 7800
```

Archival consequence:

> Boundary gating improved search economy, not invention quality.

## Surviving contraction

The benchmark history does **not** support the claim:

> Negative-Space Search creates better ideas or autonomously discovers missing representations.

The surviving contribution is:

> **The surviving contribution is search governance rather than superior discovery.**

More precisely:

> A system with an explicit representational adequacy-boundary mechanism can detect demonstrated inadequacy and conditionally allocate expansion effort, reducing unnecessary search in the controlled v0.7 benchmark while preserving the repair quality of the matched generic synthesizer.

## Null remains live

> **Matched general search systems may achieve equal or better repair quality at equal or lower total cost without explicit adequacy-boundary structure.**

Future evaluation should attack this null rather than assume the current architecture is uniquely necessary.

## Archival rule

Do not hide failed stronger hypotheses.

Do not reinterpret later benchmark success as retroactive proof of claims that earlier discriminators rejected.

The narrowing from discovery to search governance is an empirical result of the program, not a wording change made for presentation.
