# v0.5 Preregistration — Basis Failure and Conditional Expansion

Status: frozen before v0.5 learner implementation.

## Research question

Can failure under a supplied representational basis be localized to the basis itself, and can the system expand that basis only when warranted?

The target transition is:

```text
resolved failure history
-> current-basis adequacy test
-> basis inadequacy diagnosis
-> conditional relation construction
-> held-out evidence-selection improvement
```

rather than:

```text
resolved failure history
-> globally search every more-complex relation
```

## Hypotheses

### H1 — basis inadequacy is identifiable

For `interaction_probe`, no single relation in the frozen initial basis will reach balanced accuracy `0.90` on the training history.

For the three control probes, at least one supplied single relation will reach or exceed `0.90`.

### H2 — conditional construction repairs the failure

After detecting the inadequate probe, the gated learner will construct:

```text
pair0_close AND pair1_far
```

or an exactly equivalent conjunction under the frozen candidate set.

### H3 — repair transfers

The gated learner will achieve higher held-out evidence-selection accuracy than the fixed single-basis selector and will correctly reject constituent-only false positives.

### H4 — gating has value beyond brute force

The always-compose learner may match transfer performance, but the gated learner will use lower candidate-search cost because it expands only the inadequate probe family.

### H5 — no negative-space identity claim

No separate negative-space-labelled learner is included. v0.5 tests generic basis repair. A positive result must not be cited as evidence of a uniquely negative-space algorithm.

## Frozen threshold

```text
BASIS_ADEQUACY_THRESHOLD = 0.90
```

No threshold tuning after CI execution.

## Frozen construction language

Initial operators:

```text
hint_high
hint_low
hint_near_zero
pair{i}_close
pair{i}_far
pair{i}_sign_disagree
```

Expansion operator:

```text
AND(a, b)
```

with `a != b`, no nesting.

The constructor may not add OR, XOR, NOT, arithmetic expressions, learned embeddings, or arbitrary Python expressions after seeing the result.

## Frozen evaluation split

Training history contains four probe families. Numeric values are jittered within relation-preserving ranges.

Held-out history contains:

```text
H_INT_1
H_INT_2
H_DEP_ONLY
H_PAY_ONLY
H_TOPOLOGY
H_NOVEL
```

`H_DEP_ONLY` activates `pair0_close` without `pair1_far` and must not be classified as `interaction_probe`.

`H_PAY_ONLY` activates `pair1_far` without `pair0_close` and must not be classified as `interaction_probe`.

`H_NOVEL` activates none of the selected probe relations and should route to `model_disrupting_probe`.

## Noise

v0.5 includes numeric measurement jitter and misleading surface hints. It does not yet include corrupted resolving-probe labels.

This distinction is preregistered to prevent later description of ordinary numeric variation as label-noise robustness.

## Primary metrics

No composite score. Report:

- basis inadequacy detection;
- false inadequacy on control probes;
- constructed relation identity;
- held-out evidence selection;
- interaction transfer;
- constituent-only false positives;
- novel-case model check;
- candidate search cost;
- final representation cost.

## Claim if positive

The strongest allowed claim is:

> A learner can conditionally expand a supplied relation basis by composing existing primitives after the current basis fails an explicit adequacy test, and the repaired basis can improve held-out evidence selection at lower search cost than indiscriminate composition search.

## Claim if null

If the always-compose learner is equally cheap, if the single basis is already adequate, or if conditional repair does not transfer, do not add new operators to rescue v0.5. Record the failure and localize whether it belongs to the adequacy criterion, construction language, case design, or the basis-repair hypothesis.
