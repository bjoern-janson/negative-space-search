# Claim Boundaries — Negative-Space Search v0.7

Status: **frozen archival claim boundary**.

This document separates what the v0.1–v0.7 benchmark sequence supports from stronger interpretations that remain unearned.

## Core frozen claim

> Negative-Space Search does not currently demonstrate superior invention. Its supported contribution is conditional allocation of representational search: determining when existing explanatory structures are demonstrably inadequate and when expansion is unwarranted.

Operationally:

```text
Psi allocates attention / representational search effort.
Evidence allocates epistemic authority.
```

A search decision can license investigation or expansion. It does not make a proposed representation true.

## SUPPORTED

### Adaptive representational search economy

In the controlled v0.7 benchmark, explicit adequacy-boundary gating preserved the repair quality of a matched generic synthesizer while reducing unnecessary candidate evaluation.

### Adequacy-boundary detection

The benchmark lineage demonstrates controlled cases in which a system can distinguish:

- ordinary unresolved uncertainty;
- a repairable basis failure inside the current construction language; and
- demonstrated non-identifiability of the current construction language.

### Conditional expansion control

When current representational structure is demonstrably inadequate, the system can conditionally allocate additional synthesis/search effort rather than expanding indiscriminately.

### Reduced unnecessary search under controlled benchmarks

In v0.7:

```text
boundary-gated candidate evaluations = 5200
always-expand candidate evaluations  = 7800
```

Both systems achieved the same invention quality and held-out transfer. The supported advantage is therefore search allocation/economy, not better invention.

## NOT ESTABLISHED

The experiments do **not** establish:

- autonomous discovery;
- discovery of truth;
- autonomous invention of representational primitives;
- operator invention without supplied primitive terminals/constructors or a supplied synthesis mechanism;
- superiority over strong generic search, representation-learning, causal-discovery, Bayesian structure-learning, AutoML, or program-synthesis systems;
- real-world effectiveness outside the controlled synthetic benchmark lineage;
- a unique negative-space mechanism;
- open-ended interface invention;
- causal truth of synthesized candidate coordinates.

## Primary null

> **Matched general search systems may achieve equal or better repair quality without explicit adequacy-boundary structure.**

This null remains live.

v0.7 does not reject it with respect to invention quality: the boundary-gated and always-expand generic synthesizers produced the same retained abstractions, the same held-out repair quality, and the same final representation cost.

## Authority boundary

A candidate abstraction earns only the authority supported by the evidence that identifies it.

```text
possibility != authority
candidate != claim
Psi != U
```

The release therefore must not be cited as evidence that Negative-Space Search discovers truth, generates intrinsically better ideas, or solves autonomous interface invention.

## Frozen interpretation

The surviving contribution is best described as:

> **search governance: evidence-conditioned control over when a representational search space should be challenged or expanded.**
